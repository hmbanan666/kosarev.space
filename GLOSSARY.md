# kosarev.space

The professional, product and author work presented on Nikolai Kosarev's site.

## Language

**Publication**:
A dated author contribution to Writing, with one identity across its language versions.
_Avoid_: Treating notes, digests and articles as separate content entities.

**Publication type**:
The author's classification of a publication as a note, digest or article, independent of its length.
_Avoid_: Deriving type from word count.

**Publication revision**:
A saved version of a publication's complete bilingual text, classification and related work.

**Published revision**:
The publication revision selected for readers. Saving another revision does not itself replace it.

**Note**:
A publication centered on a personal observation or an engineering thought.

**Digest**:
A publication bringing together developments or discoveries with primary sources and author commentary.

**Article**:
A publication developing a subject through a sustained explanation or technical investigation.

**Writing**:
The single chronological feed of author publications of every type.
_Avoid_: Separate note, digest and article sections.

**Project**:
An independent product or body of product work, whether active or historical.

**Case**:
A focused account of an engineering problem, responsibility, decisions and outcome.
_Avoid_: Treating a case as a publication merely because it contains prose.

**Related work**:
A genuine relationship from a publication to a project or case, without changing either entity's identity.
